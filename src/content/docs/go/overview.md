PayIn Go is the PayIn product line for face-to-face and in-person merchant payment flows.

It is separate from the online payment gateway model used by PayIn Cloud and PayIn Open.

## Typical scenarios

- Small merchant in-person payment acceptance
- Point-of-sale style payment requests
- QR-code payment flows
- Lightweight merchant operations

## Product boundary

PayIn Go should not be documented as a deployment mode of PayIn Open or PayIn Cloud. It is its own product line optimized for offline and face-to-face merchant workflows.
