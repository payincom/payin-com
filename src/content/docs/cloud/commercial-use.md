PayIn Cloud is intended for merchants who want a hosted stablecoin payment service with PayIn-operated infrastructure.

Commercial onboarding normally covers business fit, supported payment flows, integration readiness, production controls, and support expectations.

## Topics to confirm

- Business use case and expected payment volume
- Supported countries, customers, chains, and tokens
- Sandbox testing plan
- Production launch timeline
- Support and incident contact path
- Fee model and commercial agreement
- Compliance and risk requirements

## Cloud vs Open decision

Choose PayIn Cloud when you want PayIn to operate the infrastructure.

Choose PayIn Open when you want to self-host and take operational responsibility for deployment, monitoring, and production risk.
